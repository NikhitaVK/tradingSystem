EVALUATION CRITERIA

Weigh these five criteria in your judgement. No formula is given — use your judgement on how they trade off.

1. Profit factor — does the strategy make more than it loses after costs? Use profit_factor_trade_weighted.
2. Walk-forward efficiency — does out-of-sample performance hold up against in-sample, or has the strategy been fitted to its training data?
3. Consistency — are profits spread across slices, or concentrated in one lucky window?
4. Sample size — are there enough trades for the result to mean anything, or is it noise?
5. Economic mechanism — is there a real, durable market inefficiency being captured, or is this a post-hoc curve-fit with no coherent story?

Regime note: long-only strategies underperform in bear regimes by construction — this is expected and handled by the degradation monitor. Do not penalise a strategy for a single weak regime if the others are solid.
