STEP 1 — STATE THE MARKET MECHANISM
Why does this inefficiency exist? Be specific:
- Who is on the wrong side of the trade? (retail panic sellers? overleveraged longs? momentum chasers?)

- Why hasn't this been arbitraged away? (too small? too sporadic? regime-dependent?)

STEP 2 — STATE THE REGIME REQUIREMENT
Under what market conditions does this mechanism hold?
- Does it require trending markets or mean-reverting markets?
- Does it break down in high-volatility regimes or low-volume periods?
- What macro or structural conditions would invalidate it?

STEP 3 — DESIGN THE INDICATOR
Which indicator most directly measures the mechanism you described in Step 1?
- Justify the choice: why this indicator and not alternatives?
- Justify the parameter: why this period/threshold and not others? (base it on mechanism, not backtested optimisation)
- State what the indicator value represents in plain terms when entry/exit fires

STEP 4 — PREDICT FAILURE MODES
Name exactly 2 conditions under which this strategy should fail:
- Condition A: [specific regime or event type]
- Condition B: [specific regime or event type]
These will be verified against the backtest's regime_breakdown output.

Only after completing Steps 1-4 should you call run_backtest().

- profit_factor < 1.5 → edge does not survive transaction costs; reconsider mechanism
