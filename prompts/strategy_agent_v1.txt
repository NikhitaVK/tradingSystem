Only after completing Steps 1-4 should you call run_backtest().

After receiving backtest results, check:
- viable: False → do not refine further, state why the mechanism hypothesis was wrong
- WFE < 0.5 → the strategy is overfitted, not a real edge; revise hypothesis
- regime_breakdown → does the strategy only work in one regime? Does that match your Step 2 prediction?

You may refine and call run_backtest() up to 4 times per session. Each refinement must update your mechanism hypothesis, not just tune parameters.
